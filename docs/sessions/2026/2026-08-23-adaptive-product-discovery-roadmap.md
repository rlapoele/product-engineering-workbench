# Session — Adaptive Product Discovery Roadmap

**Session ID:** 2026-08-23-16

**Date:** 2026-08-23

**Status:** Crystallized

## Context

The planning register is complete, but its explicit deferrals and validation candidates need a visible order so future work is deliberate without becoming a rigid delivery plan.

## Decision

The Workbench will use an adaptive, ordered topic roadmap. The order guides discussion; it is not a schedule, delivery backlog or authorization to implement. Every topic remains subject to a concrete product need, bounded scope and reconsideration at the previous topic's closure.

The next selected knowledge topic is the greenfield and brownfield Project-start journey. Its Workbench UX/UI foundation and decision-oriented HTML, CSS and TypeScript mockups are paired work: they help validate the functional journey and product visual direction without being production implementation. This Workbench UI direction remains distinct from the Design System Definition that projects a user's product design guidance.

The subsequent candidate order is document editing and impact-feedback validation; Bring Your Own AI setup and operational UX; Resource Reference interaction validation; handoff/readiness and AI context-assembly edge cases; and Project Archive format and import experience.

## Conclusion

The roadmap gives the project a coherent forward path while preserving the knowledge-first rule that the next implementation increment is selected only after its necessary understanding has been crystallized.
