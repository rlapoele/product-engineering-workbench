# Session — Project Foundation

**Date:** 2026-07-15

**Session ID:** 2026-07-15-01

**Status:** Crystallized

---

## Context

Initial exploration of the Product Engineering Workbench.

The objective of this session was to establish the project's foundations before beginning implementation.

---

## Key Insights

- The workbench should prioritize **helping users think** rather than simply generating documentation.
- Product knowledge should be represented as interconnected artifacts rather than monolithic documents.
- Conversations are valuable for exploration but should eventually crystallize into stable project knowledge.
- The repository itself should become the project's long-term memory.

---

## Decisions

- Adopt a **knowledge-first engineering** approach.
- Separate repository documentation into:
    - Knowledge
    - Planning
    - Sessions
    - Glossary
- Distinguish **Product Engineering** from **Product Delivery**.
- Limit the Product Engineering Workbench's scope to Product Engineering.
- Treat AI as an optional contributor rather than a prerequisite.
- Adopt a document-first user experience centered around a single product document.

---

## Open Questions

- Is **Product Engineering** the most appropriate name for the knowledge-production domain?
- How should workflow templates evolve as the product matures?
- How should knowledge provenance be represented throughout the workbench?
