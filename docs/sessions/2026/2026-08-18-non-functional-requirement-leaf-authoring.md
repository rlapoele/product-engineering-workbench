# Session — Non-Functional Requirement Leaf Authoring

**Session ID:** 2026-08-18-20

**Date:** 2026-08-18

**Status:** Crystallized

## Context

Quality requirements must remain brief enough to scan in a document while retaining the information needed to interpret a measurable expectation accurately.

## Decision

- A Non-Functional Requirement has a required concise title and required measurable requirement statement.
- The statement is canonical and independently interpretable.
- Optional Measurement context may clarify a measurement event, conditions, performance baseline, relevant source or standard.
- Measurement context informs interpretation only. It does not replace Acceptance Criteria or become a test plan.

## Consequences

NFRs can render as readable formal specification entries while still carrying the context needed to prevent ambiguous measurements. Explicit Acceptance Criteria remain the reusable evidence model for validation.
