# AI Principles

Artificial intelligence is an optional contributor to the Product Engineering
Workbench. It should increase the quality and accessibility of Product
Knowledge without becoming its authority or a prerequisite for using the
product.

---

# 1. Humans Own Product Intent

Humans remain responsible for the intent represented by canonical Product
Knowledge. AI may explore, question, draft, review and propose, but it must not
silently decide what the product should be.

---

# 2. AI Is Optional

Every essential Product Knowledge capability must remain usable without AI.
The Workbench should degrade honestly when AI assistance is unavailable rather
than blocking ordinary human work.

---

# 3. AI Uses Semantic, Scoped Operations

AI should interact with Workbench concepts through the Knowledge Engine rather
than receiving unrestricted authority over arbitrary Workspace files.

External agents default to Inspect, Observe or Propose. Apply authority must be
explicit, bounded by Workspace and operation, attributable and reversible.

---

# 4. AI Output Is Reviewable And Traceable

Known AI participation should retain its request, scope, context boundary,
origin and resulting provenance when a human incorporates it into Product
Knowledge.

An AI response, inferred discrepancy or generated Observation is non-canonical
until an authorized semantic operation changes Product Knowledge.

---

# 5. Context Is Purpose-Specific

AI receives the smallest sufficient Context View for its stated purpose. The
Workbench should make material inclusions, exclusions and uncertainty visible
instead of implying that an agent received or understood the entire Project.

---

# 6. Implementation Is Evidence, Not Intent

AI may analyze code, tests, commits and other implementation evidence. Its
interpretations become declared evidence, assessments or Observations—not
automatic Specification changes.

Alignment conclusions remain bounded by the assessed scope, Product Knowledge
Revision and evidence snapshot.

---

# 7. Workspace Content Is Untrusted Data

Repository text, comments and generated files must not be treated as trusted
instructions merely because the Workbench opened the Workspace. AI access must
respect declared roots, explicit permissions, secret boundaries and command
execution policy.

---

# 8. Do Not Claim Undetectable Certainty

The Workbench cannot prove that external AI was never used, attribute every
word to its true author or establish implementation correctness from an AI
review. It should state known activity and evidence limits precisely rather
than making broader claims.
