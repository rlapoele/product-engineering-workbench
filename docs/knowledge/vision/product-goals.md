# Product Goals

This document defines the primary goals of the Product Engineering Workbench.

These goals guide product decisions, prioritization and long-term evolution.

---

# Primary Goal

Help individuals and teams produce high-quality, implementation-ready product knowledge.

---

# Core Goals

## G-001 — Improve Product Thinking

Help users better understand problems, explore alternatives, challenge assumptions and make informed product decisions.

Success means users think more clearly rather than simply producing more documentation.

---

## G-002 — Improve Product Knowledge

Help users produce product knowledge that is:

- complete;
- consistent;
- coherent;
- traceable;
- implementation-ready.

The workbench should continuously encourage improvements to product knowledge.

---

## G-003 — Reduce Ambiguity

Help users identify:

- missing information;
- inconsistencies;
- conflicting requirements;
- unanswered questions;
- hidden assumptions.

The workbench should increase confidence before implementation begins.

---

## G-004 — Preserve Knowledge

Capture important product knowledge in a structured and maintainable form.

Knowledge should remain understandable and reusable long after the original discussions have ended.

---

## G-005 — Support Human Thinking

Artificial intelligence should augment human reasoning without replacing human judgment.

The workbench should encourage critical thinking and informed decision-making.

---

## G-006 — Produce Implementation-Ready Artifacts

Enable users to generate artifacts that can be confidently consumed by:

- software engineers;
- QA engineers;
- software architects;
- AI implementation agents;
- AI testing agents;
- AI reviewing agents.

---

## G-007 — Remain Methodology-Agnostic

Support different product engineering approaches through configurable workflow templates.

The workbench should not require users to follow a single methodology.

---

## G-008 — Integrate with Existing Ecosystems

Allow product knowledge to be exported or integrated with external tools used during Product Delivery.

The workbench complements existing delivery platforms rather than replacing them.

---

# Success Indicators

The Product Engineering Workbench succeeds when users can:

- think more effectively;
- produce better product knowledge;
- reduce unnecessary ambiguity;
- collaborate more effectively;
- confidently begin implementation;
- reuse and evolve product knowledge over time.

---

# Non-Goals

The Product Engineering Workbench does not aim to become:

- a project management platform;
- a sprint planning tool;
- a task management system;
- a source code management platform;
- a software delivery platform.

These activities belong to Product Delivery and are intentionally outside the primary scope of the workbench.
