Product overview

MealLoop is a meal planning and discovery app that helps people eat well with less stress. It brings together meal ideas, planning, and grocery coordination so everyday food choices require less effort.

It gives individuals and households a calmer way to decide what to cook, make use of what they already have, and build routines that work in real life.

Goals and success criteria

This section defines the outcomes that guide the product and make progress observable.

  • Improve mealtime simplicity: help people plan and prepare meals with less effort.
  • Reduce food waste: make better use of ingredients already available.
  • Build repeatable routines: support confident planning over time.

Target users

MealLoop is for busy individuals and households who value healthier eating but need practical support fitting food planning into daily life.

Problem or opportunity

People make repeated meal decisions with incomplete information about time, ingredients, preferences, and household needs.

Solution summary

Provide a flexible product experience that turns small decisions into an understandable weekly plan.
